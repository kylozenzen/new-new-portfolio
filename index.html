The useful longer version

I’ve worked across destination brands, tech education, architecture, and SaaS. I can lead the strategy, write the copy, shape the campaign, manage the calendar, work the comments, coordinate with stakeholders, and help a good idea survive contact with the real world.

I also build small tools when a marketing workflow is clunky. That work strengthens my social-media practice through audience empathy, product thinking, experimentation, and a better understanding of the technology behind modern content teams.

Off the clock: girl dad, husband to a zookeeper, theme-park person, gamer, and firm believer that “professional” does not have to mean “written by a committee of beige cardigans.”

How I work

Start with the goal

Tie attention to awareness, trust, action, revenue, or a clearer brand position.

Respect the platform

Keep the brand recognizable without copy-pasting one idea into every feed.

Build useful systems

Create planning rhythms and reusable formats that leave room for timely creativity.

Learn and iterate

Measure what changed, own what did not work, and make the next idea smarter.
