How I work

Make the invisible work visible.

The best coordination happens before a missed deadline becomes a fire drill. I’d rather surface the question, owner, dependency, or risk while it’s still boring.

01

Listen first.

Understand the actual goal, the people involved, and what “done” needs to mean before turning the conversation into tasks.

02

Translate clearly.

Turn client and internal conversations into notes, decisions, owners, timelines, and next steps that people can actually use.

03

Create the rhythm.

Keep status updates, check-ins, project systems, and follow-ups consistent enough that the team knows where things stand.

04

Watch the edges.

Look for the missing asset, unclear approval, slipping dependency, client question, or tiny detail that can become tomorrow’s problem.

05

Close the loop.

Review, confirm, communicate, deliver — and make sure the project does not quietly die three inches from the finish line.
